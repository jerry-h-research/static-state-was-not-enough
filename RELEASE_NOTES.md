## Reproducibility boundary
The Claim Lifecycle source snapshot is not a self-contained 23-suite reproduction package because the historical `tests/` and `phase2_tests/` corpus is not included.

## Excluded
Raw private transcripts, private study screenshots/image contents, personal identifiers, account details, credentials, local machine paths, third-party personal information, the full historical test corpus, full raw Q2 trace/transaction files, and unredacted historical Project US material.

## Author
Jerry H. — Independent Researcher

## Main result
A frozen 496-word static coordination packet did not establish practical coordination benefit in PCB-1. Separately, a heavily tested Claim Lifecycle implementation produced no measurable downstream advantage over a lightweight baseline on the frozen Formal Q2 slice (7/7 vs 7/7). The broader open question is whether useful human-AI coordination depends partly on ongoing calibration that static state representations fail to preserve.
