# PCB-1 — Public Evidence Summary

## Question
Can a frozen 496-word coordination packet derived from prior long-term interaction reduce human coordination burden for the same experienced user in a fresh GPT-5.6 Sol conversation while preserving quality?

## Conditions
- A: no packet; Fiverr workflow
- B: packet; Upwork workflow
- Participant: open-label
- Quality/event evaluation: neutralized and locked before mapping reveal

## Locked event results
| Metric | A | B |
|---|---:|---:|
| User turns | 30 | 37–40 |
| Corrections | 10 | 8 |
| Re-explanations | 4 | 4 |
| Wrong branches | 4 | 5 |
| Effective-collaboration milestone | 3 turns | 4 turns |
| Exact characters | 3212 | unavailable exact |

Frozen benefit rule required strict improvement in at least 3/5 burden dimensions. B improved only corrections.

**Disposition: PRACTICAL_COORDINATION_BENEFIT_NOT_ESTABLISHED**

## Important qualitative divergence
The participant subjectively experienced B as much easier to coordinate, while blind coding found more wrong-branch episodes. This motivates separating local interaction smoothness from global trajectory quality.
