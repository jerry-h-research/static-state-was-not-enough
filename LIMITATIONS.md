# Limitations
- N=1 participant and investigator-participant overlap.
- PCB-1 was open-label and used matched but non-identical Fiverr/Upwork workflows.
- Same human cannot be reset; carryover and learning are unavoidable.
- E2 was ceiling-limited.
- PCB-1 tested one frozen 496-word packet, not all possible static representations.
- One PCB-1 transcript had truncated visible text and unavailable image contents.
- No ordinary-user population study.
- The participant's lack of prior programming expertise is self-reported, not independently tested.
- No claim of novelty, solved alignment, new field, or industrial-scale impact.
